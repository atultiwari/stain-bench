# H&E protocol shared by all three versions

Sketchbook (`sketchbook/`), Turntable (`turntable/`) and Working Model (`working-model/`) all follow this one list:
the same stations, the same names on the jars, the same order and the same lab times.

**Source:** Bancroft's *Theory and Practice of Histological Techniques*, 8th ed.
- Ch. 10, p. 131, box "Non-automated hematoxylin and eosin stain for paraffin sections", steps 1–10.
- Ch. 10, p. 128: Harris's hematoxylin used regressively, 5–15 min.
- Ch. 11, Fig. 11.5: a typical linear stainer: xylene → alcohols → water → hematoxylin → water → acid alcohol →
  water → Scott's tap water → water → eosin → water → alcohols → xylene.

Spelling: British (haematoxylin), as on the pages. Bancroft uses the American spelling.

## Steps (7 buttons)

| Step | Stations | What it does (Bancroft) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Dewax | 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 | Xylene removes the wax; descending alcohols take the section to water |
| 2. Haematoxylin | 6 → 7 | Alum haematoxylin overstains; the tap-water wash starts to blue it |
| 3. Differentiate | 8 → 9 | 1% acid alcohol removes excess dye; nuclei keep it but turn **red** (acid) |
| 4. Blue | 10 → 11 | Weak alkali turns the nuclei from red to blue-black |
| 5. Eosin | 12 → 13 | Eosin stains cytoplasm pink and red cells orange-red; **the tap-water wash differentiates the eosin** |
| 6. Dehydrate and clear | 14 → 15 → 16 | Alcohols remove water (a little more eosin comes out); xylene clears |
| 7. Coverslip | 16 → 17 | One slide at a time, still wet with xylene: a drop of DPX, coverslip lowered from one edge |

## Expected results (Bancroft p. 131)

Nuclei blue/black · cytoplasm varying shades of pink · muscle fibres deep pink/red · red blood cells orange/red · fibrin deep pink.

## Corrections to the earlier pages

- After eosin there **is** a running tap-water wash (1–5 min). The earlier "no water rinse, it would wash the eosin out" was wrong.
- Eosin is **10 min** (1% eosin Y), not 1–2 min.
- Alcohol strengths use Bancroft's wording: **99%** (absolute), 95%, 70%.
- The bluing jar is labelled **SCOTT'S** (Scott's tap water substitute), not "BLUING".
- 17 stations, not 16 (one more tap-water wash, after eosin).
