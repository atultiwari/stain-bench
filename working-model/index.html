Sources

Every station, name and time follows the shared protocol (he-protocol.md): Bancroft's Theory and Practice of Histological Techniques, 8th ed., ch. 10, p. 131 (non-automated H&E, steps 1–10) and p. 128 (Harris haematoxylin, regressive, 5–15 min); ch. 11, Fig. 11.5 (jar order of a linear stainer). Stated by Bancroft: haematoxylin 5 min, tap water until blue 5 min, acid alcohol 10 s, Scott's then a 5 min wash, eosin 10 min then a 1–5 min wash (2 min here). Typical times where Bancroft gives none: 2 Xylene 5 min, 3 99% (absolute) alcohol 2 min, 4 70% alcohol 2 min, 5 Water 1 min, 9 Running tap water 1 min, 14 95% alcohol 1 min, 15 99% (absolute) alcohol 2 min, 16 Xylene (clearing) 5 min. Lab clock = bath time only, 47 min 10 s; carrying the rack and mounting are not counted. pH values are approximate general chemistry. Stain uptake curves are a teaching model tuned to the textbook result, not measurements.
